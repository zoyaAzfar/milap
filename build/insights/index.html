We can see that coverage is uneven: Other Lahore alone accounts for 11 of the 68 facilities, while 23 other neighborhoods — including Aitchison Society, Architects Engineers Housing Society, Aziz Bhatti Town, Bahria Town, Bankers Cooperative Housing Society, DHA, and more — have just one. On average a facility is 1.3 km from its nearest neighbor, but 8 have no other listed facility within 3 km — the most isolated being Aziz Jahan Begum Trust Institute for Blind, Lahore; Nexus School for Special Children; Roots & Wings Autism Center; Lahore Autism Center. The listing farthest from the city center, Roots & Wings Autism Center, is about 28.9 km out. Distances are straight-line and measured from an approximate central point in Gulberg, so real travel times will be longer.

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is named by 30 of 68 facilities, and the top 5 categories each have 19 or more. At the other end, categories such as Behavioural Disorders, ADHD, Cerebral Palsy (CP), Down Syndrome, Developmental Disorders, Learning Challenges / Slow Learners (SL) are each named by at most 15 facilities in the directory which is a a real gap for those families, though a few facilities list general labels like "All Disabilities (Inclusive)" and may accept these children too.

Disability Inclusive & Learning Lahore, or DIL Lahore, is an independent, non-ranked directory of special-education and disability services in Lahore, compiled from public listings and facility records. Details can change, so confirm fees, hours, and admissions with a facility before visiting.
